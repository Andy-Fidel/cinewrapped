## Decision change process

1. Describe the observed problem and supporting measurement.
2. List at least one viable alternative and migration impact.
3. Record privacy, security, data, operational, and rollback consequences.
4. Add a superseding ADR entry with its effective date.
5. Update the architecture, tests, operational runbooks, and implementation checklist together.
